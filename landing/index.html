Questions

Do we need new hardware?

No. It runs in the browser on the iPad, phones and TV you already have. For the TV, any smart TV browser or a cheap streaming stick works.

How long does setup take?

Most stores are live the same day. We load your roster and past sales, you sign in on the store iPad, and you open TV mode on the kitchen screen.

Where does the forecast come from?

Your own sales. Once a week you upload the 15-minute sales report your POS already exports, and the forecast rebuilds itself for every day of the week.

Is our store data safe?

Your data sits in your store's own secure database. Only the logins you approve can see it, and none of it is stored in the app's code.

Can my team actually use it?

Simple mode was built for a shift leader in the middle of a rush: big buttons, three steps. Advanced mode is there when you want to plan deeper.

What happens after the pilot?

If it's earning its keep, keep going on a monthly plan. If not, walk away. You don't owe anything and there's no contract.
