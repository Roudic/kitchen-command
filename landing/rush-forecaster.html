Questions

What sales report do I need?

The sales-by-15-minute export your POS already makes, saved as a CSV. Upload it once a week and the forecast rebuilds itself.

How much history does it need?

It works from a single week, but it flags that as low confidence. With three or four weeks it's solid, and it keeps learning as you upload.

What about holidays and catering days?

Partial days are left out, and a spike cap stops one huge order from inflating next week's forecast.

Can I set my own crew numbers?

Yes. The bands and the BOH count for each band follow your store's staffing chart.

Can I upgrade later?

Any time. Your forecast carries straight over into Kitchen Command.
